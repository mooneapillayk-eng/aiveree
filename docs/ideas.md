## Platform + Varenza governance cross-sell
_Parked 2026-10-27_

**The idea**
- Open Aiveree up as a **platform other people build products on** (leaning on the
  Build studio and the Act execution engine).
- When what someone builds **is an AI product**, offer **Varenza** as the layer
  they use to **govern** that product (AI governance / oversight / compliance).
- Make **Varenza a connector** in Aiveree's connectors marketplace, so a builder
  can one-click add governance to a product they've built on Aiveree.

**Why it's worth keeping**
- Two complementary layers: **Aiveree = build**, **Varenza = govern**. Natural
  cross-sell and a recurring governance revenue stream alongside the build side.
- Reinforces Aiveree's trust positioning (the "on your side / safe" wedge): the
  products built on it come with governance available by default.

**Revisit when**
- We design the connectors/marketplace platform, and the "build on Aiveree"
  surface (third-party builders), so Varenza can slot in as a first-class
  connector at that point.

**Open questions for later**
- What exactly Varenza governs and how it plugs in (API/SDK/policy hooks).
- Whether governance is opt-in per product or a condition of building AI on the
  platform.
- Pricing: bundled, per-seat, or usage-based governance.
